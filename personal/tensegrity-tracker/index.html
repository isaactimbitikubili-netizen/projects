🧠 Neurological baseline Autonomic state first. Your nervous system dictates capacity — if it's depleted (ADHD burnout is common), heavy load causes regression, not adaptation. Measure before you load.

A. Morning check — HRV / RHR on waking

⚛️ First principle: HRV & resting HR reflect sympathetic vs. parasympathetic balance. Trend matters, not one morning. A personal rise in RHR (~+7–10 bpm over your 7-day average) or HRV dip + fatigue = system asking for down-regulation, not intensity.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: single mornings are noisy (bad sleep, heat, dehydration, alcohol, late caffeine, illness). Never judge one reading. Need 7+ mornings. Same time, same position (sitting/lying), before phone/coffee.

B. 10-min silence sit — dopamine / stillness capacity

⚛️ First principle: can you sit in a dark, silent room 10 min without phone or stimming? This probes baseline arousal + interoception. Difficulty ≠ personal failure — it's information that the system currently needs parasympathetic practice before load-bearing.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: ADHD/ASD brains find stillness harder by design. Score urges, don't moralize them. Track urge intensity over weeks. Environment matters (noise, hunger, heat).
🧩 Metacognition prompt: "What did I feel at minute 2 vs. minute 8? What strategy did I use — breath, noticing, counting?" You're training observation, not perfection.

C. Interoceptive heart test — vagal gating

⚛️ First principle: feeling your heartbeat without touch = interoception + vagal tone. For ADHD/ASD this connection supports emotional regulation. Post-exercise signal usually appears before resting signal — that's expected order, not failure.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: track confidence 1–10 over time, not yes/no verdicts. Anxiety, caffeine, noise all gate the signal.

🏗️ Biomechanical baseline Don't test 1-rep max. Test structural integrity: vestibular + proprioception + fascial elasticity + joint centration.

A. Blind single-leg stand — vestibular + proprioception

⚛️ First principle: eyes closed removes vision; balance must come from inner ear + foot proprioceptors + cerebellum. Under ~15s suggests foot/ankle foundation work first. Test barefoot, safe space, arms crossed.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: time varies with fatigue, surface, shoes, illness, heat. Always test same conditions. Compare left vs. right and weekly range, not one wobble.

B. Deep primal squat — fascial elasticity + ankle dorsiflexion

⚛️ First principle: heels-down deep squat tests posterior-chain fascial elasticity + ankle dorsiflexion + hip health. New baseline goal: 5 min comfortable (v1 was 3 min — your old logs still count).
🔍 Scrutiny cue: depth and comfort change with warm-up, footwear, fatigue, ankle stiffness in the morning. Support (doorframe) is a valid progression, not cheating.
00:00
Feet shoulder-width. Support is allowed while building up.

C. Dead hang — grip + shoulder centration

⚛️ First principle: max hang tests grip (correlates with nervous-system health) + shoulder joint centration. Hang from a secure bar, active shoulders (don't shrug to ears), breathe.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: grip varies hugely with sweat/heat, bar thickness, skin, fatigue. Log conditions. Stop on sharp shoulder pain — discomfort in forearms is expected, joint pain is a stop signal.
00:00

D. Wall angel — thoracic + shoulder centration

⚛️ First principle: back to wall (heels, glutes, upper back, head). Goalpost → Y without low-back arching. Tests thoracic extension + pec length + deep neck flexors ("upper-cross" from desk/tech).
🔍 Scrutiny cue: morning stiffness vs. post-walk differ. Compare same time of day. Partial contact is a starting point with a clear progression.

🌿 Biological baseline Environmental integration. In Uganda's equatorial heat, hydration + minerals + sleep are usually the bottleneck — fatigue is more often cellular hydration failure than lack of fitness.

⚛️ First principle: sleep architecture + digestion + water/electrolytes gate everything above. Fix order on fatigued days: water → electrolytes → shade/rest → sleep tonight → train tomorrow. Don't interpret performance before checking these.
🔍 Scrutiny cue: heat, sweat, alcohol, late heavy meals, and poor sleep explain most "I got worse" days. Log them so your brain can't skip to self-blame.
🧩 Metacognition prompt: "If a friend showed me this log, would I say they're unfit — or hot, tired, and under-hydrated?"

🧭 First-principles tutor How to think, not just what to log. Built for deliberate metacognition without spirals.

The stack (order matters)

  1. 🧠 Neuro first: morning RHR/HRV + stillness + interoception. If the nervous system is depleted, load causes regression. Prescription: down-regulate (slow exhale, walk, shade, sleep) before barbells.
  2. 🏗️ Structure second: balance → squat → hang → wall angel. No 1RM until joints centre and feet sense the ground. Under ~15s blind stand = foot/ankle foundation. Short hang + shoulder pain = hang less, scap work more.
  3. 🌿 Biology always: sleep + water + electrolytes + digestion. Equatorial rule: if fatigued, check hydration/minerals before fitness story.

Ask like a first-principles thinker (copy these into Notes)

  • System: what system am I actually testing — arousal, vestibular, fascia, grip, hydration?
  • Confounders: sleep? heat/sweat? water/lytes? stress/illness? caffeine? time of day? surface/bar?
  • Base rate: what's my 7-day range? Is today inside it (= noise) or outside it twice in a row (= signal)?
  • Smallest next test: what's the tiniest change that would test my hypothesis (e.g. +0.5L water + lytes for 3 days)?
  • What would falsify my worry? "If my RHR is back within 3 bpm after 2 good nights, it was fatigue, not decline."

Decision tree — what do I do today?

  1. RHR +7–10 over your average or fatigue ≥7 or sick? → Restore: water + lytes, shade walk, early bed. No hard training. Log and release.
  2. Otherwise, blind stand <15s? → Foundation: barefoot balance + calf/ankle + short supported squats.
  3. Otherwise, water <2L in heat or no lytes + headache/fatigue? → Rehydrate: water + electrolytes / salty soup + fruit before training.
  4. Otherwise → Build: practise one quality: squat depth, hang time, wall angels, or stillness.

ADHD/ASD-friendly logging rules

  • Good-enough > perfect: 60 seconds, estimates fine. Round numbers are data.
  • Same conditions: same time/place/surface when you can — reduces noise your brain will otherwise catastrophize.
  • One verb, one number: don't log everything daily. Rotate: e.g. Mon morning+balance, Wed squat+hang, Fri silence+bio.
  • Close the loop: after saving, close the tab. No re-reading 5×. Set one check-in day per week for trends.
  • Language swap: "failed / dysregulated / fried" → "lower than my range / system asking for rest / loaded week." Words shape spirals.
